# Seller-pricing glossary

## Individual seller

A person in Indonesia intending to list a second-hand electronic item that they own. This is the intended user, not a verified ownership status.

## Comparable evidence

A marketplace listing that has passed the product identity, variant, condition, currency, duplication, and outlier checks required by the valuation engine.

## Recommended listing-price range

The weighted 25th through 75th percentile range calculated from accepted comparable evidence. It covers the item price only.

## Suggested listing price

The weighted median of accepted comparable evidence. It is the recommended starting price for a balanced sale, not a guarantee of sale price or speed.

## Listing location

An optional city or region supplied by the seller. It affects evidence selection only and does not create an AI-generated regional price adjustment.
