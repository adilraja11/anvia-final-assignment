# Seller-pricing glossary

## Individual seller

A person in Indonesia intending to list a second-hand electronic item that they own. This is the
intended user, not a verified ownership status.

## Comparable evidence

An identity-matched Blibli listing that has passed product identity, variant, price, currency,
availability, duplication, and outlier checks. Lifecycle and listing condition do not determine
whether a listing is comparable.

## Suggested listing price

The deterministic median of at least five accepted Blibli advertised asking prices. It is a
recommended starting price for a balanced sale, not a guarantee of sale price or speed.

## Observed market range

The unweighted 25th through 75th percentile range of the same accepted Blibli evidence. It is
displayed as **"Rentang harga pasar saat ini di Blibli"** and is not tailored to the submitted
item's condition.

## Confidence

`HIGH` requires at least 15 accepted listings. `MEDIUM` requires five through 14. Fewer than five
accepted listings returns `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE` rather than a price recommendation.

## Listing location

An optional city or region supplied by the seller. It affects evidence selection only and does not
create an AI-generated regional price adjustment.
